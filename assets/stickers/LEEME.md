# Stickers

Put your own images here, as PNG with a transparent background. Each one shows up on its own;
while a file is missing, its spot simply stays hidden.

| File             | Where it appears                              | Suggested size |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------|----------------|
| `portada.png`    | Cover, sitting on the bottom-right of the photo | ~600 px wide |
| `formulario.png` | Peeking over the top of the RSVP form         | ~600 px wide   |
| `cierre.png`     | Closing, next to the cake                     | ~700 px wide   |

The white die-cut border and the entrance animations are added by the page.
